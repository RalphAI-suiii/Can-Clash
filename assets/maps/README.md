# Arena floor artwork

Generated with built-in Imagegen on 2026-10-24 for the existing Can Clash cartoon
style. The 1536×1024 images are flat floor surfaces, without baked obstacles or UI.
They fill the same fenced play area; cover and scenery remain separate scene nodes.

- `eskinita_floor.png`: light repaired concrete slabs, subtle blue-gray patches,
  shallow seams and narrow edge drains. Style reference: `ground_toon.png`.
  Generated image: exec-a440c803-b0b9-40f2-90f1-59d03574ecba.png.
- `dusty_yard_floor.png`: warm ochre packed earth, soft worn paths and small flat
  pebble/leaf details, with a quiet central throwing lane. Style reference:
  `dirt_patch_1.png`. Generated image: exec-0b0323e8-876b-4d95-ab14-c8794544c8fe.png.

Prompts required a directly overhead view, clean hand-painted cartoon shading,
no photographic grain, no objects/characters, no text or sports markings. Artwork
is intentionally distinct from the basketball asphalt. Existing house, laundry,
palm and plant assets supply perimeter scenery; they do not add invisible blockers.
