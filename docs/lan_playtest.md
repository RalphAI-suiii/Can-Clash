# Five-PC LAN acceptance checklist

Run this checklist when the relevant gameplay is implemented. It has not been run.

- [ ] One host and four clients join a five-player lobby.
- [ ] The throw-off selects the initial Taya under the agreed tie rule.
- [ ] Four throwers can aim, charge, and throw simultaneously.
- [ ] Slippers ricochet off intended objects without affecting players.
- [ ] A later bounce can earn a successful hit on a standing can.
- [ ] Miss points are awarded once under the agreed miss rule.
- [ ] Players retrieve their own slippers and score safe returns once.
- [ ] Two personal can hits power the next throw.
- [ ] Taya picks up, carries, and resets the can at its base.
- [ ] Carrying slows Taya and prevents lunging.
- [ ] Taya cannot tag until the can is reset or inside the safe strip.
- [ ] Contact and lunge tags transfer roles and award points once.
- [ ] Lunge observes its 10-second cooldown.
- [ ] Points stay with players across role changes.
- [ ] All PCs agree on the timer and final rankings.
- [ ] Disconnects follow the agreed match policy.
- [ ] Invalid or unauthorized client requests cannot alter match outcomes.
